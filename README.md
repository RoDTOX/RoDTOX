## 📌 About Me

- 🔧 **Core Embedded & IoT:** Hands-on experience developing bare-metal firmware, FreeRTOS tasks, and custom device drivers on **STM32** and **ESP32**. Deep focus on low-power architectures, BLE/BTHome telemetry, and industrial/automotive communication protocols (**CAN**, **MQTT**).
- 🤖 **Autonomous AI & Tooling:** Designing agentic developer tools, local AI orchestration pipelines, and vision-assisted monitoring systems that automate repetitive engineering tasks.
- 📐 **Engineering Discipline:** Practical advocate of Test-Driven Development (TDD), MISRA-C guidelines, deterministic state machines, and hardware reverse-engineering.
