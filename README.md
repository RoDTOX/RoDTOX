## 📌 Background & Identity

### 🚗 Industrial & Automotive Core (11+ Years)
- **Automotive Communication Middleware:** Extensive hands-on experience designing and validating vehicular networks (**CAN, CAN-FD, Automotive Ethernet**), lower-layer communication drivers, and deterministic frame dispatching.
- **Microcontroller Architectures:** In-depth work on automotive-grade targets, including **Infineon AURIX TriCore** (TC3xx/TC4xx) and **Qualcomm Automotive SoCs**.
- **Vehicle Simulation & HIL:** Engineering **CANoe Restbus Simulations (RBS)**, automated CAPL test frameworks, and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) environments under **ASPICE** and safety-oriented methodologies.
- **Architectural Standards:** Designing modular systems strictly adhering to **ISO 11898** and **MISRA-C:2012** constraints (see the public [CAN Scheduler Software Detailed Design](https://github.com/RoDTOX/rodtox.github.io)).
